When to go

Late May to June 2027: warm weather, busy hostels and lower prices than midsummer.

  • Early fall is the alternative, with autumn colours along The Ocean's route.
  • Book The Ocean first, since it runs only three days a week.
  • Use VIA's Escape fares for the city-to-city trains.
  • Watch for youth discounts for ages 18 to 24, like the Canada Strong Pass.
  • Book hostels on their own websites for the best rates.
